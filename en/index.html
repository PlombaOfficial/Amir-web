How we’ll work

From idea to launch

You don’t need to know anything about websites. Just tell me about your business — I’ll handle the rest.

  1. 01

    We talk free

    You tell me about your business and your customers. I ask questions and give you a straight answer: what kind of site you need, what it will cost and how long it will take.

  2. 02

    I show you how it will look

    Before any building starts, you see the site as a design. We adjust colours, photos and the order of sections until you’re happy. I only move on once you say yes.

  3. 03

    I build it

    This part is on me. You can open the link at any point to see how it’s coming along — and say if you’d like something changed.

  4. 04

    I launch it

    I put the site online, connect your address (something like your-cafe.kz) and test everything across different phones and computers.

  5. 05

    I stay in touch

    The first month after launch is free: change prices, add photos or a promotion. After that, whenever you need me.
